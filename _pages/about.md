**Email:** tewodrosayalew [at] uchicago [dot] edu

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Chicago, advised by [Dr. Matthew Walter](https://home.ttic.edu/~mwalter/) at the [Robot Intelligence through Perception Laboratory (RIPL)](https://ttic.edu/ripl/) at the Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC).

My research focuses on **world models** and **latent actions** learned from diverse, unlabeled video, with **reward learning** from visual observations as a complementary thread. In [CLAW](https://ripl.github.io/claw/), I jointly learn a diffusion-based world model and continuous latent actions end-to-end from action-free video; the resulting model supports imitation from observation and goal-directed planning, and I evaluated it on a real UR5 with pretraining on human video (e.g., 43% RoboDesk upright-block planning vs. 5% AdaWorld). In [PROGRESSOR](https://ripl.github.io/progressor/) (ICCV 2025), I learn a self-supervised visual progress reward with online refinement for robot RL without manual reward design. I also co-authored a [CoRL 2026 Oral](https://ripl.github.io/manipulation_benchmark_audit) on what robot manipulation benchmarks actually measure. I am interested in scaling these directions beyond curated datasets toward in-the-wild visual experience.

Prior to my PhD, I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Saskatchewan, where I worked with [Dr. Ian Stavness](https://www.cs.usask.ca/faculty/stavness/) on unsupervised computer vision.
